How we selected

A useful Spanish shortlist, not a catalogue

We checked the current title, final URL, Spanish location, organiser, programme focus, public price, reviews, availability and calendar for every selected listing. Marketplace order was used only for discovery, not as an objective ranking.

Before booking

  • Confirm the exact room, dates, inclusions and cancellation terms.
  • Ask which Pilates apparatus is used and how often sessions take place.
  • Tell the organiser about injuries, mobility needs or dietary concerns before paying.
  • Review fasting details carefully for the Lanzarote option.
  • Check flights, transfers and travel insurance separately.
  • Re-open the listing: marketplace details can change quickly.

Common questions

Which retreat is the strongest first choice?

Tenerife is the most balanced option for a broad audience because Pilates is personalised, the calendar is flexible and the listing has the deepest review base among the Pilates-centred choices.

Which is the most affordable?

Dénia had the lowest public starting price at the 31 August check, but it is tied to 2–5 October 2026 and showed only three spots. Price and availability may change.

Which options are most focused on Pilates?

Tenerife, Dénia, Lanzarote and Marbella present Pilates as a central part of the stay. Mallorca and Fuerteventura combine it with a broader yoga and wellness schedule.

Why is Sitges not included?

The live listing description referred to an autumn retreat while the displayed booking calendar showed 23–28 March 2027. We excluded it rather than recommend an internally inconsistent offer.

Do the prices include flights?

No assumption should be made. The figures shown are public starting prices for the retreat listing; travel and other extras may not be included. Verify the current booking page.

Our current pick

Tenerife is the primary recommendation: personalised Pilates, flexible dates and 43 reviews make it the most balanced starting point. Dénia is the value choice for travellers who can use the fixed October dates; Lanzarote is the stronger activity-led alternative for those who specifically want walking and the stated fasting structure.
